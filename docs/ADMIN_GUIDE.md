# IDENT AFRICA Admin Guide

## Overview

Welcome to the IDENT AFRICA Business Owner Command Center. This guide will help you manage your safari booking platform without any technical knowledge.

---

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Admin Panel

1. Go to your website URL followed by `/admin`
2. Log in with your credentials
3. You'll see the Command Center dashboard

### Navigation

The admin panel is organized into sections:

- **Command Center** - Overview and quick actions
- **Content** - Manage website pages and sections
- **Bookings** - Handle reservations
- **Customers** - View customer information
- **Marketing** - Promotions and campaigns
- **Settings** - System configuration

---

## Command Center Dashboard

The Command Center gives you a real-time view of your business.

### Key Metrics

| Metric | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Today's Bookings | Number of new bookings today |
| Pending Approvals | Bookings waiting for confirmation |
| Today's Revenue | Money received today |
| Customer Enquiries | Questions from potential customers |

### Quick Actions

From the dashboard, you can quickly:

- **Add Destination** - Create a new safari destination
- **Create Package** - Set up a new safari itinerary
- **View Bookings** - See all reservations
- **Generate Report** - Create sales analytics

## Managing Content

### Website Control Center

The Content Manager lets you update your website without touching code.

#### Pages

1. Go to **Content** → **Pages**
2. You'll see all your website pages:
   - Homepage
   - Destinations
   - Accommodation
   - Experiences
   - Packages
   - About
   - Contact

3. Click on any page to edit its settings

#### Homepage Sections

Control what appears on your homepage:

1. Go to **Content** → **Homepage Sections**
2. You'll see sections like:
   - Hero Banner
   - Featured Destinations
   - Safari Experiences
   - Safari Packages
   - Customer Reviews

3. **Enable/Disable**: Click the eye icon to show or hide a section
4. **Edit**: Click the pencil icon to modify section content
5. **Reorder**: Drag sections to change their order

#### Adding Content

1. Go to **Content** → **Content**
2. Choose what to create:
   - **Destinations** - Safari locations
   - **Experiences** - Safari activities
   - **Accommodation** - Lodges and camps
   - **Packages** - Safari itineraries
   - **Gallery** - Photos
   - **Testimonials** - Customer reviews

3. Click **Add new** on the type you want
4. Fill in the form
5. Click **Save**

## Managing Bookings

### Booking Operations Center

All reservations come through here.

#### Viewing Bookings

1. Go to **Bookings** in the menu
2. You'll see tabs:
   - **All Bookings** - Every reservation
   - **Pending** - Waiting for confirmation
   - **Confirmed** - Approved bookings
   - **Completed** - Finished trips

#### Booking Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | New booking, needs review |
| Confirmed | Approved, awaiting payment |
| Paid | Payment received |
| Completed | Trip finished |
| Cancelled | Booking cancelled |

#### Approving a Booking

1. Click on a booking to open details
2. Review the customer information
3. Click **Update Status**
4. Select **Confirmed**
5. Click **Save**

#### Contacting a Customer

From the booking details:

- **Send Email** - Click the email icon
- **Call** - Click the phone icon
- **Add Note** - Keep internal notes

## Managing Customers

### Customer Management

View all your customers and their history.

#### Customer Information

Each customer profile shows:

- Contact details (email, phone)
- Total bookings
- Total money spent
- Booking history
- Communication history

#### Sending Messages

1. Click on a customer
2. Go to the **Communications** tab
3. Click **Send New Email**
4. Write your message
5. Click **Send**

## Marketing Center

Promote your safari business.

### Creating Promotions

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Promotions**
2. Click **Create Promotion**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** - "Summer Safari Sale"
   - **Code** - "SUMMER20" (customers use this)
   - **Type** - Percentage or Fixed amount
   - **Value** - How much discount
   - **Dates** - When it works

4. Click **Create Promotion**

### Promotion Types

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Percentage | 20% off |
| Fixed | $500 off |

### Managing Campaigns

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Campaigns**
2. Create ad campaigns
3. Track:
   - Impressions (people who saw it)
   - Clicks (people who clicked)
   - Conversions (people who booked)

## Settings

### Brand Settings

Customize your brand:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Brand**
2. Upload your logo
3. Set brand colors
4. Write your tagline

### Business Settings

Configure operations:

1. **Currency** - What customers pay in
2. **Tax Rate** - Percentage added to bookings
3. **Booking Rules**:
   - Minimum days before trip
   - Maximum days ahead to book
   - Cancellation notice period

### Payment Settings

Enable payment methods:

1. **Stripe** - Credit cards (recommended)
2. **PayPal** - PayPal payments
3. **Bank Transfer** - For large bookings

### Security

Protect your account:

1. **Session Timeout** - Auto-logout time
2. **Two-Factor Authentication** - Extra login security
3. **API Keys** - For developers

## Media Library

### Accessing Media

1. Go to any content section

3. Browse existing images

### Uploading Images

## Troubleshooting

### Can't log in?

1. Check your email/password
2. Try resetting your password
3. Contact technical support

### Booking not showing?

1. Check spam folder
2. Verify booking was completed
3. Contact support

### Payment not processing?

1. Check payment settings
2. Verify Stripe is connected
3. Contact support
