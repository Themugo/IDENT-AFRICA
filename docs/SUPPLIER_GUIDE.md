# IDENT AFRICA Supplier Guide

## Overview

Welcome to the IDENT AFRICA Supplier Marketplace. This guide helps suppliers manage their products, bookings, and grow their business on our platform.

---

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Supplier Portal

1. Go to your registration confirmation email
2. Click the login link or visit `/supplier`
3. Use your credentials to access your dashboard

### Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard shows:
- **Revenue** - Total earnings and monthly trends
- **Bookings** - Incoming reservations
- **Products** - Your safari packages and experiences
- **Notifications** - Important updates

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Company Information

Provide your business details:
- Company name
- Business type
- Description
- Location

### Step 2: Document Upload

Upload required documents:
- Business registration certificate
- Operating license
- Insurance certificate
- Tax identification

### Step 3: Payment Details

Set up your payment receiving:
- Bank name
- Account number
- SWIFT code

### Step 4: Submit Application

Review and submit for review.

### Step 5: Wait for Approval

Our team reviews applications within 2-3 business days.

## Managing Your Profile

### Updating Company Info

1. Go to **Profile** tab
2. Edit company name, tagline, description
3. Upload cover image and logo
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Best Practices for Profile

- **Logo**: Use a professional logo (square, min 200x200px)
- **Cover Image**: Use a stunning landscape photo (3:1 ratio)
- **Description**: Tell your unique story
- **Location**: Be specific about your area

---

## Managing Products

### Creating a New Product

1. Go to **Products** tab
2. Click **Add Product**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: Clear product name
   - **Description**: What makes it special
   - **Duration**: Days and nights
   - **Price**: Per person rate
   - **Location**: Where it operates
   - **Images**: High-quality photos

### Product Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Package | Complete safari itinerary |
| Accommodation | Lodge rooms, camps |
| Experience | Activities, day trips |
| Transport | Transfers, vehicle rental |
| Meal Plan | Dining options |

### Setting Pricing

Consider these factors:
- Operating costs
- Platform commission (set by admin)
- Market competition
- Seasonality

### Managing Availability

- **Always Available**: For fixed offerings
- **On Request**: You confirm before booking
- **Limited**: Set specific slots

## Handling Bookings

### Viewing Bookings

1. Go to **Bookings** tab
2. See all reservations in one list
3. Filter by status or date

### Booking Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Awaiting your confirmation |
| Confirmed | You accepted the booking |
| Completed | Trip finished |
| Cancelled | Booking cancelled |

### Confirming a Booking

1. Click on booking to view details
2. Review customer requirements
3. Click **Confirm Booking**
4. Customer receives notification

### Managing Customer Requirements

Note any special requests:
- Dietary needs
- Accessibility requirements
- Group size changes

## Reviews & Ratings

### How Reviews Work

Customers who complete bookings can leave reviews.

### Review Components

- **Overall Rating** (1-5 stars)
- **Title** - Brief summary
- **Comment** - Detailed feedback
- **Pros/Cons** - What they liked/didn't

### Responding to Reviews

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Find the review
3. Click **Respond**
4. Write your professional response

### Building Your Reputation

- Respond to all reviews
- Address concerns professionally
- Request reviews from happy customers
- Maintain consistent quality

## Payments & Payouts

### Commission Structure

IDENT AFRICA charges a platform fee:
- Standard: 15%
- Rates may vary by supplier

### Getting Paid

1. Bookings are processed through the platform
2. Payments are held until booking completion
3. Payouts are released after trip completion
4. Funds transfer to your bank account

### Payment Schedule

- **Net 30**: Standard payout terms
- Payouts processed on the 1st and 15th

### Viewing Earnings

1. Go to **Overview** tab
2. See total revenue
3. Check monthly breakdown

## Security & Permissions

### What You Can Do

✅ **Manage your products** - Create, edit, archive
✅ **Handle bookings** - Confirm, modify, cancel
✅ **Update profile** - Change info and images
✅ **View earnings** - Track revenue
✅ **Respond to reviews** - Engage with customers

### What You Cannot Do

❌ **Access competitor data** - Privacy protection
❌ **View customer payment info** - Security requirement
❌ **Modify platform settings** - Admin only
❌ **Access other suppliers** - Isolated environment

### Keeping Your Account Secure

- Use a strong password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Never share login details
- Log out on shared devices

## Troubleshooting

### Can't log in?

1. Check your email/password
2. Try password reset
3. Contact support

### Product not showing?

1. Check status is "Active"
2. Verify all required fields filled
3. Contact support

### Payment not received?

1. Check booking status is "Completed"
2. Verify bank details are correct
3. Contact support

### Booking not appearing?

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Verify booking was confirmed
3. Contact support
